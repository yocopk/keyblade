# Contributor License Agreement

Keyblade requires every contributor to agree to this CLA before a pull request can
be merged. The check is automated; you will be prompted on your first PR.

## Why

Without a CLA, copyright in the project becomes shared among everyone who has ever
contributed. That has one practical consequence worth being upfront about: the
project could never change licence, offer a commercial licence, or relicense a
component, without tracking down and getting agreement from every past contributor.
Projects have been frozen permanently by exactly this.

With a CLA, the project keeps one clear owner who can make those decisions, while
you keep full ownership of your own work.

widely understood and has been tested in practice.
